# Writing the cover letter

## Shape (250-350 words, one page)
1. **Opening (2-3 sentences):** the role, and one specific reason this company and role fit the candidate: something from the posting or the company's product, tied to something the candidate has done. Mention a referral by name here if the user agreed.
2. **Evidence (1-2 paragraphs):** the 2-3 requirements that matter most in the posting, each answered with a concrete achievement from the CV (numbers, scale, outcome, link). Show, don't claim.
3. **Fit and logistics (2-3 sentences):** why this team or problem, plus the practical facts the employer will wonder about, stated plainly and positively: location and relocation, work authorisation (the user's visa line for that country), start date.
4. **Close (1 sentence):** invite a conversation; name the attached CV.

## Style
- The user's voice: direct, specific, plain words; short sentences. Read their CV summary and any letter they have written to match it.
- Name the actual product, team or technology from the posting; generic letters lose.
- Gaps: do not apologise; show the nearest evidence and how quickly they learn (with an example).
- Avoid: "I am writing to express my interest", "passionate", "team player", "fast-paced", "I believe I would be a great fit", restating the CV line by line, flattery.
- Never invent experience, numbers, motivations or relationships. If a sentence needs a fact you do not have, ask.

## Language and format
- English by default. German if the posting is in German and the user wants it: "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren," (or "Sehr geehrte/r Frau/Herr <Name>,"), formal "Sie", close "Mit freundlichen Grüßen". The user's German level must match what the letter implies; if the letter is German but their German is B1, say so in the letter or keep it in English.
- Salutation: a named person if the posting or `contacts.md` gives one; otherwise "Dear <Company> team," / "Dear Hiring Team,".
- Date, the candidate's contact block, and the company line at the top (the LaTeX template handles layout).

## Checklist before marking final
- Every requirement claimed is supported in `fit.md`.
- Company name, role title and any person's name are spelled as in the posting.
- Length one page; no word from the never-mention list.
